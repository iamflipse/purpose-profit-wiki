Resilience in particular

'Resilient' appears only once, in a list of traits (p. 42). The book mostly covers the idea in other words: persist and iterate, error correction, the anxious buffer period, years of failure.

  • 25. The Wrong Question and Self-Governance: "To be resilient, teachable, open-minded, perceptive, creative, adaptable, and everything else that separates the caged from the free."
  • 1. Not a Practical Business Book: "I want to provide you with a frame from which you can create your own actionable steps, and when those fail, you will have a mind built to persist and iterate until they don’t."
  • 39. Boredom, Anxiety and Flow: "The first leap into a new way of life will have a buffer period of high anxiety. This is nature’s way of testing how serious you are about seeing what you’re capable of."
  • 62. The Author's Path: "With persistence, those failures turned into clarity."
  • 11. Stepping-Stones and Complacency: "You are required to be okay with failure, rejection, and slow progress. You are required to learn from your mistakes, show up again tomorrow, and push until you strike gold."
  • 64. Humans Took Over the Role of Creator: "Although problems become more complex, you become more equipped with knowledge, skill, and experience to solve them."

Discipline in particular

The word 'discipline' does not appear. The book covers it as standards, levers (daily priority tasks), consistency and self-mastery:

  • 48. Mission, Standards and Goals: "You aren’t where you want to be because you are okay with where you are."
  • 48. Mission, Standards and Goals: "You don’t need motivation when the task in front of you is so stupidly simple that you can’t help but complete it."
  • 49. Projects, Constraints and Levers: "Every day, you need priority tasks that move the lever toward your projects, goals, and vision from the ground up."
  • 43. Getting Paid to Be Yourself: "The good life demands consistent effort toward reaching a new level of purpose."
  • 25. The Wrong Question and Self-Governance: "Self-mastery—an unwavering dedication to the process of navigating reality."
  • 58. How to Write: "This is where I publish no matter how I feel about the writing because I know it is not optional for the ideal future I want to sustain."
