## Resilience in particular

'Resilient' appears only once, in a list of traits (p. 42). The book mostly covers the idea in other words: persist and iterate, error correction, the anxious buffer period, years of failure.

- [25. The Wrong Question and Self-Governance](essays/25-self-governance.md): "To be resilient, teachable, open-minded, perceptive, creative, adaptable, and everything else that separates the caged from the free."
- [1. Not a Practical Business Book](essays/01-not-a-practical-book.md): "I want to provide you with a frame from which you can create your own actionable steps, and when those fail, you will have a mind built to persist and iterate until they don’t."
- [39. Boredom, Anxiety and Flow](essays/39-boredom-anxiety-flow.md): "The first leap into a new way of life will have a buffer period of high anxiety. This is nature’s way of testing how serious you are about seeing what you’re capable of."
- [62. The Author's Path](essays/62-authors-path.md): "With persistence, those failures turned into clarity."
- [11. Stepping-Stones and Complacency](essays/11-stepping-stones-and-complacency.md): "You are required to be okay with failure, rejection, and slow progress. You are required to learn from your mistakes, show up again tomorrow, and push until you strike gold."
- [64. Humans Took Over the Role of Creator](essays/64-humans-as-creators.md): "Although problems become more complex, you become more equipped with knowledge, skill, and experience to solve them."

## Discipline in particular

The word 'discipline' does not appear. The book covers it as standards, levers (daily priority tasks), consistency and self-mastery:

- [48. Mission, Standards and Goals](essays/48-mission-standards-goals.md): "You aren’t where you want to be because you are okay with where you are."
- [48. Mission, Standards and Goals](essays/48-mission-standards-goals.md): "You don’t need motivation when the task in front of you is so stupidly simple that you can’t help but complete it."
- [49. Projects, Constraints and Levers](essays/49-projects-constraints-levers.md): "Every day, you need priority tasks that move the lever toward your projects, goals, and vision from the ground up."
- [43. Getting Paid to Be Yourself](essays/43-getting-paid-to-be-yourself.md): "The good life demands consistent effort toward reaching a new level of purpose."
- [25. The Wrong Question and Self-Governance](essays/25-self-governance.md): "Self-mastery—an unwavering dedication to the process of navigating reality."
- [58. How to Write](essays/58-how-to-write.md): "This is where I publish no matter how I feel about the writing because I know it is not optional for the ideal future I want to sustain."
