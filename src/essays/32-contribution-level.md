# 32. 4. Contribution

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Levels of Purpose  
**Book pages:** pp. 59 to 60  

## Summary
Contribution is the second half of creation: art merged with business so the art takes full effect. The domains of your life collapse into one, and work, rest and play become hard to tell apart. You become a perspective vessel who gathers, synthesizes and distributes information. The levels are worldviews, not rules, and you can contribute or create at any level.

## Key ideas
- Contribution is the second half of creation.
- Art must be merged with business to take full effect.
- The separate domains of life collapse into one.
- Work, rest and play become hard to distinguish.
- You gather, synthesize and distribute information; you become less of a leech.
- The four levels are general worldviews; you can contribute or create at any stage.
