# 28. Problems Are the Path

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Levels of Purpose  
**Book pages:** pp. 49 to 50  

## Summary
Under the book's own subheading, Koe argues you can only become as successful as the identity you expand into, and problems are the limit on that expansion. Problems box in your attention, so you can't see your passions. Accepting your situation is a band-aid. To solve a problem you zoom out, set a goal that opposes it, collect ideas, and experiment until a new level of purpose sets in.

## Key ideas
- You can only become as successful as the identity you expand into.
- Problems are the limits on your potential.
- Each solved problem makes life feel easier and raises your level of purpose.
- Problems box your attention, hiding passions and interests.
- Accepting your situation is a band-aid.
- We find joy in what we choose to suffer for.
- Zoom out, create an opposing goal, form a theory, experiment until solved.
