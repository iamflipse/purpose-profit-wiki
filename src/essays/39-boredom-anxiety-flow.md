# 39. Boredom, Anxiety and Flow

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Progress and Knowledge  
**Book pages:** pp. 71 to 73  

## Summary
Psychic entropy comes from boredom (skill too high for the challenge) and anxiety (challenge too high for the skill). Boredom breeds self-centeredness and anxiety breeds self-consciousness. Staying in the known breeds boredom, while leaping into the unknown brings a buffer period of anxiety that tests how serious you are. The key is to dance between them with challenges just above your level: flow.

## Key ideas
- Psychic entropy comes from boredom and anxiety: a skill-challenge mismatch.
- Boredom leads to self-centeredness; anxiety to self-consciousness.
- Spiraling thoughts stop when you refocus on what you can do now.
- Most lives are determined by how people cure boredom.
- Stepping into the unknown is a rebirth with a buffer period of high anxiety.
- Reverse entropy by taking challenges just above your skill level.
- Flow: living at the edge of your abilities; maximize time there.
