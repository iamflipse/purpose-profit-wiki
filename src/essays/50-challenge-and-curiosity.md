# 50. Challenge and Curiosity

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Your Life’s Work  
**Book pages:** pp. 88 to 89  

## Summary
Challenge is the source of enjoyment, which sits on the tightrope between boredom and anxiety. Problems aren't the bane of your existence but the reason for it. Curiosity keeps you from locking into the routine you were trying to escape, and your vision runs like a battery fueled by experience, education and misdirection. Run the whole process whenever you're lost.

## Key ideas
- When novice plays master, neither has fun.
- When skill matches challenge, the world goes quiet.
- Challenge is the source of enjoyment.
- Problems aren't the bane of your existence; they're the reason for it.
- Be curious, steer off course, avoid idolized paths.
- Vision is a battery fueled by experience, education and misdirection.
- Run the process when lost, when relationships fail, when business stalls.
