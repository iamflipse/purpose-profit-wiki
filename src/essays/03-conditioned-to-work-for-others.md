# 3. Conditioned to Work for Others

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Truth About Jobs  
**Book pages:** pp. 7 to 8  

## Summary
The Truth About Jobs opens with the claim that school silently teaches you to build other people's dreams. Assigned work done without struggle makes you mechanical and replaceable, and a cloud of responsibilities eats the time, energy and money you could use to change your life.

## Key ideas
- School encodes a hidden lesson: your purpose is to build their dreams.
- Assignments lead down a known path, not a new one.
- Work done without struggle or conscious thought makes you mechanical and replaceable.
- You learn things you don't care about to prepare for a life you don't care about.
- Time, energy and money get exhausted like clockwork, so you stay a useful worker.
- If you hate your work, controlling your day is the top priority.
