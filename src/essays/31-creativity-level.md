# 31. 3. Creativity

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Levels of Purpose  
**Book pages:** pp. 57 to 59  

## Summary
Pursuing status opens the mind. Spirituality comes from the journey toward a conscious destination. Once status is reached, material things get old, but nothing was wasted. In the creativity level you build a philosophical mastery around a few pursuits, see the patterns across methods, and create your own way. Your job is simply to create and experiment.

## Key ideas
- Start the business to make money; it's the only way to control what and whom you create for.
- Spirituality comes from the journey, which needs a conscious destination.
- Once status is achieved, the money, cars and watches get old.
- Nothing was a waste; you have a vat of experience to pass down.
- Creativity level: break from external dependencies; build mastery in a few pursuits.
- Integrate the finite game and fall in love with the infinite.
- Experiment, break from dogma, build novel solutions from scratch.
