# 58. How to Write

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Meta Skill  
**Book pages:** pp. 110 to 112  

## Summary
Writing is accessible: you already write every day and don't need a degree. Start at rock bottom, which is liberating, because you get to be a fool while nobody watches. Koe outlines first, lets the outline frame how he sees the world, adds ideas like brush strokes, then publishes however he feels about it. Writing explores idea space when thinking falls flat. Write every morning with your ideal future in mind.

## Key ideas
- You already write every day; no degree needed.
- Start at the start: rock bottom, absolute zero.
- Writing is daunting only when you project too far into the unknown.
- Writers are DJs with ideas.
- Outline first, let it sit and frame your perception.
- Publish regardless of how you feel; perfection is for the unsuccessful.
- Writing explores idea space and stores ideas so you find new ones.
- Write every morning with your ideal future in mind.
