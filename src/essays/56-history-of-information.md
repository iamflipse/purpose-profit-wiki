# 56. Information, Identity and Mental Slavery

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Meta Skill  
**Book pages:** pp. 105 to 107  

## Summary
Koe traces information from touch to sight to memory to writing, which took a step toward universality. Information is the code of your mental operating system: the goals you adopt and the ideas you collect form your identity. Centralized institutions once injected society's goals into you, and the default was mental slavery. The internet decentralizes ideas, and the ideas holding the most mental real estate will shape humanity.

## Key ideas
- Information gathering evolved from touch to sight, sound, memory and consciousness.
- Writing took information toward universality and acts as collective memory.
- The internet frees people from institution-controlled information.
- Information is the code of your mental operating system.
- Goals and ideas form an interconnected web we call identity.
- Centralized information made mental slavery the default.
- The ideas holding the most mental real estate will determine humanity's outcome.
