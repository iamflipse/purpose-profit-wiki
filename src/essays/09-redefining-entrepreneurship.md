# 9. Redefining Entrepreneurship as Agency

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Employment vs Entrepreneurship  
**Book pages:** pp. 16 to 17  

## Summary
Entrepreneurship and business have become dirty words thanks to unquestioned beliefs. Koe redefines the employee and entrepreneur difference as low versus high agency: whether you create your own goals or are assigned them. True agency means blaming yourself for every problem, even when you're not at fault.

## Key ideas
- People shut off their minds at the words entrepreneurship and business because of programming.
- Common false beliefs: only for talented people, needs big money, full of unethical people.
- Employee vs entrepreneur is low agency vs high agency.
- High-agency people create their own goals and pursue them without permission.
- Low-agency people pursue assigned goals because they can't see another option.
- True agency means owning every problem, even ones that aren't your fault.
