# 61. One-Person Media Companies: Justin Welsh

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Self-Monetization  
**Book pages:** pp. 117 to 119  

## Summary
Starting a business doesn't take capital, warehouses or an LLC. Media on the internet, with writing as the core skill, is a meta path that doesn't saturate. People become one-person media companies. Justin Welsh left a high-pressure executive role, wrote about his skills and story, built an audience, then sold consulting and a self-paced course, building a business around his life.

## Key ideas
- Starting a business doesn't need big capital.
- The internet and writing are a meta path that doesn't saturate.
- People are becoming one-person media companies.
- Justin Welsh: executive burnout, writing, audience, consulting, a course.
- He didn't quit outright; he shared his skills, opinions and story.
- His story makes his skills desirable to those who relate.
- Build a business around your life, not a life around your business.
