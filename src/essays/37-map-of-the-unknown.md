# 37. The Map of the Unknown

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Progress and Knowledge  
**Book pages:** p. 69  

## Summary
Picture the unknown as a map with light and dark spots, where your potential sits in the dark. Ideas get tested against reality, and when they fail, a problem is revealed and you error-correct. The problem of all problems is that people rarely dive into the unknown. Without a goal, good problems go unnoticed, and without problems there's no creativity and no life.

## Key ideas
- The unknown is a map; dark spots are where your potential lies.
- Ideas are tested against reality; failure reveals a problem to error-correct.
- Your purpose is the source of your struggle, and struggle can be fun.
- The problem of all problems: people rarely dive into the unknown.
- Conditioning, identity and perception keep you from noticing good problems.
- Without a goal, problems don't register.
- Without problems, there's no creativity; problems, like ideas, are infinite.
