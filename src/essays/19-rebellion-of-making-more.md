# 19. The Rebellion of Making More

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Unignorability of Money  
**Book pages:** pp. 31 to 33  

## Summary
If good people won't start businesses, unethical people rise unopposed, and you end up working for them anyway. Koe argues the greatest rebellion against money culture is to make more of it, with purpose. Tech now lets anyone distribute value globally, a realistic path out of a system built on useful workers. You can only pull attention from something by offering something more attractive.

## Key ideas
- If ethical people don't start businesses, unethical ones rise without competition.
- Refusing business usually means working for one that's unethical by your standards.
- The greatest rebellion against money culture is making more of it, with purpose.
- Tech-enabled distribution of value is a realistic path out of the mess.
- Doing nothing lets less conscious people hold more attention, impact and money.
- You only stop attention on one thing by offering something more attractive.
- Building a solution beats casting a tiny vote.
