# 1. Not a Practical Business Book

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Introduction  
**Book pages:** pp. 5 to 6  

## Summary
Koe opens by saying the book won't give step-by-step instructions, because his map won't fit your territory. Instead he wants to install better beliefs, a meta frame from which you can design your own steps. He also states his aim of merging purpose and profit rather than glorifying or rejecting money.

## Key ideas
- Copying someone else's exact steps fails because you don't have their mind, experience or failures.
- The goal is better ideas that frame how you think about action, not a list of actions.
- Belief comes before action, but the beliefs must not be dogmatic, so he goes meta.
- A mind built to persist and iterate is the real outcome he is after.
- Rejecting money is not the answer. The only option is to merge purpose and profit.
