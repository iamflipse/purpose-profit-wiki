# 4. Job, Career, Calling

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Truth About Jobs  
**Book pages:** pp. 8 to 10  

## Summary
Koe defines three kinds of work. A job is survival work done for someone else. A career is a commitment to development through a hierarchy of challenges. A calling is work you can't pull yourself away from and that others can't help but pay you for, found where improvement turns into obsession.

## Key ideas
- A job is unpleasant work for someone else, purely for money, a survival mechanism.
- Good marketing keeps jobs and schools alive despite bad results.
- A career is a hierarchy of challenging roles that brings order and extends schooling.
- A calling can't be assigned or limited to working hours and is found where improvement becomes obsession.
- A calling has to be protected, like a gift others could accidentally steal.
- Jobs suit the young or those surviving, careers suit those wanting satisfaction, and callings suit those willing to plunge into the unknown.
