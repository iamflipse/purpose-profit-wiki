# 20. The Labor Theory of Value

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Unignorability of Money  
**Book pages:** pp. 33 to 35  

## Summary
Hard work alone doesn't guarantee money. Degrees, ladders and books can take years and still go unpaid, because hours worked don't make anyone care. Pay tracks value: the level of problems you solve, the results you create, and your ability to make people know and care. Pay based on hours makes less sense as work gets more efficient.

## Key ideas
- Through hard work alone you can work at anything, but that doesn't make it useful.
- Hours worked don't guarantee anyone will care.
- Complaining about deserving more misses that people invest in what benefits them.
- Money correlates with the level of problems solved, results created and persuasion.
- If you're unhappy with your income, look honestly at what you contribute.
- Paying by problem level makes sense because problems evolve as work does.
