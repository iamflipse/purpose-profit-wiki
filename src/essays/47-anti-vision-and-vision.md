# 47. Anti-Vision and Vision

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Your Life’s Work  
**Book pages:** pp. 84 to 85  

## Summary
The plan starts with an anti-vision, the future you refuse to live. Koe calls it a positive-fear mechanism and suggests keeping a running note of what you never want to repeat. Then comes vision: without one you are lost, and every decision should be filtered through it. Write a minimum viable vision in thirty minutes and revise it often. For brands, the vision shows where you're going and the anti-vision gives supporters an enemy.

## Key ideas
- Anti-vision: the bane of your existence, a positive-fear mechanism.
- Keep a running note of experiences you don't want to repeat.
- Without a vision you are lost and live determined outcomes.
- Filter every decision through your vision.
- Write a minimum viable vision in thirty minutes and iterate.
- A brand's main trait is its vision; anti-vision gives supporters an enemy.
