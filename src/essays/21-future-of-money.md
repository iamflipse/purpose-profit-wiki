# 21. Enjoyment, Overflow and the Future of Money

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Unignorability of Money  
**Book pages:** pp. 35 to 36  

## Summary
Enjoyment comes from progress, connection to something greater and meaningful feedback, and conscious entrepreneurship sustains it. Money began as beads and seashells. It will keep evolving, become more digital, and may turn into attention or status as AI lowers the cost of living. Creating value stays worthwhile either way.

## Key ideas
- Enjoyment is progress plus connection to something greater plus feedback.
- Fill your own cup and you overflow into helping others.
- Money lets humans transcend survival and drives new tools.
- Money has a 40,000-year history and keeps evolving past seashells and the dollar.
- The future of money is more digital and may become attention or status.
- Solving problems and creating value remains the worthwhile path regardless.
