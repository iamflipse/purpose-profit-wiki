# 8. A Prediction About the Future of Work

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Employment vs Entrepreneurship  
**Book pages:** pp. 15 to 16  

## Summary
Koe predicts the future of work will be mostly entrepreneurs, especially creators, plus a shrinking number of elite employees with entrepreneurial traits. The entry level is going extinct. The choice is to depend on a basic income or take full responsibility. He frames this as empowering and promises a mind that can adapt to any future.

## Key ideas
- The future of work will consist mostly of entrepreneurs, specifically creators.
- Elite employees with entrepreneurial traits will hold increasingly rare positions.
- The entry level is going extinct.
- The two options are dependence on government income or full responsibility for your future.
- Headlines about AI and job replacement make the future feel dark, but the book aims to equip a mind that adapts.
