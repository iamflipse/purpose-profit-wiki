# 14. Other-Development and the Extension of Yourself

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Employment vs Entrepreneurship  
**Book pages:** pp. 22 to 24  

## Summary
An unfulfilled entrepreneur still has an employee's mind, working for an invisible employer planted by their programming. Koe defines entrepreneurship as other-development: you become valuable by solving the problems that limit you, then earn by sharing the solution with people who have the same problem. He calls it an extension of yourself and says our ancestors did it without the label.

## Key ideas
- Unfulfilled entrepreneurs serve an invisible employer left by their programming.
- Entrepreneurship is other-development, the step after self-development.
- Become valuable by solving your own limits; earn by distributing that solution.
- Nobody will live in a post-survival era; survival must be integrated meaningfully.
- Entrepreneurship is the distribution of your value and your protection against replacement.
- Ancestors served tribal roles that gave them purpose; employment isn't our natural state.
- Today's real threats are psychological and spiritual.
