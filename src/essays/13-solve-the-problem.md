# 13. Solve the Problem

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Employment vs Entrepreneurship  
**Book pages:** pp. 21 to 22  

## Summary
Koe strips entrepreneurship down: it is full control over earnings, lifestyle and suffering through solving problems. Creative adaptability never goes out of style, because problems never go away. Every complaint becomes a problem to solve, whether prioritization, self-management, perception, health or state of mind.

## Key ideas
- Entrepreneurship is not a pipedream reserved for people who work long hours.
- Its purpose is control over earnings, lifestyle and suffering by solving problems.
- True entrepreneurship is creative adaptability to any problem or opportunity.
- Problems never go away, so this never goes out of style; that should feel liberating.
- Each complaint (hours, stability, looks, mood) is a solvable problem that raises earning capacity.
