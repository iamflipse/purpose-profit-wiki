# 65. The Second Renaissance and the Role of Creators

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Become a Creator  
**Book pages:** pp. 129 to 132  

## Summary
Koe believes we are in a Second Renaissance, happening on the internet. The new society has consumers, creators and companies. 'Creator' means the essence of your being, not a content-creator label. A creator self-reflects, finds a problem, explores, tests and passes down something of value. Creators can be one-person companies, and they are becoming a decentralized education system and the sense-makers of society.

## Key ideas
- We are in a Second Renaissance, on the internet and faster.
- Three types: consumers, creators and companies.
- 'Creator' is the essence of your being, not a narrow job label.
- To create is to self-reflect, identify, explore, hunt, test and pass down.
- Creators can be one-person companies with a generalist skill stack.
- Trust in schools falls because there are more options.
- Filtering signal from noise is the high-value skill of the future.
- Creators are sense-making pillars; companies hire and train creators.
