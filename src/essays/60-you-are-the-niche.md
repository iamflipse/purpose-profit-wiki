# 60. You Are the Niche

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Self-Monetization  
**Book pages:** p. 116  

## Summary
Your niche isn't out there; you are standing in it. Self-awareness is the greatest business, marketing and sales skill. Reverse-engineer what already hooks you and rebuild it with you at the centre. Solve your own problems and sell the solution, which is self-improvement and other-improvement, purpose and profit. Your identity is unique, so conformity is a finite game and authenticity an infinite one.

## Key ideas
- Becoming the niche means it evolves as you do.
- Most people stand in their niche without realizing it.
- Self-awareness is the greatest business, marketing and sales skill.
- Reverse engineer what already hooks you, with you as the central pillar.
- Obsess over solving your own problems, not market opportunities.
- Solve your problems and sell the solution: purpose and profit.
- Conformity is a finite game; authenticity is infinite.
