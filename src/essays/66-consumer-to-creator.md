# 66. From Consumer to Creator

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Become a Creator  
**Book pages:** pp. 133 to 137  

## Summary
Creators act like boutique stores, making personal goods and services for the audience they attract. People already earn a living from problems they've solved. Creators are the decentralized education system, economy and culture. The path to being future-proof is shifting from consumer to creator: solve your problems, publish the solutions, help even 1,000 true fans. Do it all and be the generalist you were born to be. The book closes with a short author's note.

## Key ideas
- Creators are like boutique stores serving like-minded audiences.
- People earn sustainable incomes from problems they've solved.
- Creators are the decentralized education system, economy and culture.
- Becoming future-proof means shifting from consumer to creator.
- Even 1,000 true fans can support a good life.
- Your only enemy is yourself, another problem to solve.
- Do it all: write, design, market, sell, film, code.
