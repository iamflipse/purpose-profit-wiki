# 40. Nature's Compass and the Story of Your Life

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Progress and Knowledge  
**Book pages:** p. 73  

## Summary
Flow happens when attention is invested in a plan: a hierarchy of goals matched to your skill. Koe calls this Nature's Compass, which is trial and error, steering like a ship heading for a lighthouse in a storm. Start where you are but challenge yourself. Life unfolds as a story in chapters and cycles, and knowing which part you're in calms the mind.

## Key ideas
- Flow comes when attention is invested in a plan, a hierarchy of goals.
- Nature's Compass: true learning through trial and error, creation and destruction.
- Like a ship in a storm: sense where you are, admit you're off course, steer.
- Don't go to sea if you can't navigate; start where you are but challenge yourself.
- This is how you acquire an interest-based education.
- Life is a story of chapters, phases and cycles; each chapter is a macrocycle.
