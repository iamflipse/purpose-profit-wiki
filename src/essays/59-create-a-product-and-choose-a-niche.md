# 59. Create a Product, and the Trouble With Choosing a Niche

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Self-Monetization  
**Book pages:** pp. 113 to 115  

## Summary
The only way to control your income is to create a product, meaning anything you give in exchange for value. If you don't, you work for someone who does or become the product, for example of a basic income. 'Choose a niche' is the standard advice. Niches are necessary, but choosing one as if it were external leads to working for people you don't care about, solving problems you haven't experienced, studying the map rather than the territory, and staying stuck in a static box.

## Key ideas
- Create a product to control your income and life.
- A product is any creation you give in exchange for value.
- Not creating one means working for someone who does, or becoming the product.
- A niche narrows focus on the individual whose problem you solve.
- Choosing a niche as something external leads to unfulfilling work.
- Most fail by solving problems they haven't experienced: map, not territory.
- Chosen niches are static and prone to replacement.
