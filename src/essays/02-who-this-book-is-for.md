# 2. Who This Book Is For

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Introduction  
**Book pages:** p. 6  

## Summary
Koe asks the reader to read the short book twice, once for consumption and once for digestion. He names the audience: creatives, unfulfilled workers and people who fear replacement. He says the writing is lightly edited and he is open to being wrong.

## Key ideas
- The book is not for those who think abundance is bad.
- Read it twice: once to consume, once to digest.
- The audience is creatives, unfulfilled workers and those who fear replacement.
- These are people who feel they have more to give but have lost trust in the default way.
- He kept the writing close to what his mind wanted to say and is open to being wrong.
