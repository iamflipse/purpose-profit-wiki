# 10. School and the Useful Worker

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Employment vs Entrepreneurship  
**Book pages:** pp. 17 to 18  

## Summary
Children get assigned a purpose and, without high-agency parents, fall into the default script: school, job, retirement. Koe says schools exist to produce useful workers for the state, though he grants they teach useful things. He is writing to his past self, the kid who knew he was meant for more.

## Key ideas
- As children we have little control over the purpose assigned to us.
- Without high-agency parents we become subservient to the dominant paradigm.
- The school system serves the state by producing useful workers.
- Interest-based self-education frees you but may skip basics like grammar and writing.
- He writes to his past self who couldn't stand an average life.
- You can't form an accurate opinion of something you haven't experienced.
