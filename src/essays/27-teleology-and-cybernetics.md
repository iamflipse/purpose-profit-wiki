# 27. Teleology, Cybernetics and Vertical Growth

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Levels of Purpose  
**Book pages:** pp. 48 to 49  

## Summary
Teleology explains things by their goal. Cybernetics studies how systems steer toward one: act, sense, compare, correct, like a ship blown off course. Together with the Kosmos they give a non-dogmatic highest aim. Koe separates horizontal development (skills within a level) from vertical development (jumping to a new level), which often comes only after intense pain.

## Key ideas
- Teleology explains something by its purpose or goal.
- Cybernetics: systems self-regulate toward a goal by steering.
- Act toward a goal, sense, compare, steer: trial and error.
- Kosmos, teleology and cybernetics form a non-dogmatic highest order to orient life.
- Horizontal development builds skills within a level; vertical development transcends it.
- People get stuck in a level, causing psychic entropy, until pain makes them flip the switch.
