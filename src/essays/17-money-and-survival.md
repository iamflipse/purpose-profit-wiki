# 17. Money Is Intertwined With Survival

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Unignorability of Money  
**Book pages:** pp. 28 to 29  

## Summary
Money is often what holds people back from their next level, because it is bound up with modern survival. It can be a domineering master or a tool to expand care. Koe walks through why we work, go off to the woods, or get fit, and finds money as a sub-reason almost every time, because survival is integrated, not escaped.

## Key ideas
- Money is often what blocks the next level of personal development.
- It dictates almost every action because it is tied to modern survival.
- Money can be a domineering master or a tool to expand care for others.
- Spirituality isn't disconnecting from the world but contributing to it.
- Money is nearly always a sub-reason because survival is integrated, not removed.
- There's never only one reason behind an action.
