# 41. Lost, Interested, Obsessed

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Progress and Knowledge  
**Book pages:** pp. 73 to 74  

## Summary
The cycle has three phases. Lost: a goal is achieved or a job stops being novel, and boredom or anxiety signal a problem. Interested: you experiment until you know you can solve it, and it becomes your purpose. Obsessed: you can't stop learning, and a money problem leads to persuasion, then the mind, then reality itself. This is the power of an interest-based education.

## Key ideas
- The cycle: lost, interested, obsessed.
- Lost happens after achieving a goal or when a job loses novelty.
- Boredom and anxiety are signals; listening makes you curious.
- Experiment until you know you can solve the problem; it becomes your purpose.
- Obsession: your mind seeks information and you dive deeper.
- A money problem leads to persuasion, the mind and reality itself.
