# 64. Humans Took Over the Role of Creator

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Become a Creator  
**Book pages:** pp. 127 to 129  

## Summary
Creativity was once reserved for the gods, but humans learned how things work and built tools to survive anywhere. Many have lost their path and act as task-completing employees. We don't know what AI will displace, but two things are certain: problems are infinite and problems are soluble. If enjoyment is progress plus contribution, and both come from solving problems creatively, the logical aim is to become a creator.

## Key ideas
- Creativity was once reserved for the gods.
- Humans built tools to survive any environment.
- Many are employees, subservient task-completing machines.
- We don't know the future of jobs, Earth or our own actions.
- Two certainties: problems are infinite, problems are soluble.
- Happiness = progress + contribution, both from solving problems creatively.
- Life gets better as problems get harder, if you keep chaos at bay.
