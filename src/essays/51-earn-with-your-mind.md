# 51. Earn With Your Mind

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Value Creation  
**Book pages:** pp. 91 to 92  

## Summary
The internet and AI let you learn and build almost anything; a website that once needed engineers now takes a template or a prompt. What used to need resources and labor needs less and less, which frees high-agency generalists and threatens people who are themselves the tools. When anyone can create anything, getting people to care becomes the problem.

## Key ideas
- You can learn and build almost anything thanks to the internet.
- AI tools can generate a website's code and teach you to host it.
- What needed resources and labor now needs less, inching toward zero.
- Liberating for high-agency generalists; oppressive for those who are tools.
- Earn with your mind, not your time, labor or looks.
- When anyone can create anything, getting people to care is the problem.
- Make your interests interesting to others.
