# 25. The Wrong Question and Self-Governance

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Deep Generalism  
**Book pages:** pp. 41 to 44  

## Summary
Asking what skills will matter in twenty years is a low-quality question that nobody else can answer for you. The answer is taking responsibility for your future and becoming resilient, teachable, adaptable and creative. True education teaches how to release and constrain entropy. Koe lists seven forms of self-governance, and says that at the start, selfishness is selfless.

## Key ideas
- 'What skills will be relevant?' is a low-quality question others can't answer for you.
- People take the most comfortable option and accept its sacrifices.
- The end to worry is taking responsibility for your future.
- Be resilient, teachable, open-minded, perceptive, creative and adaptable.
- True education teaches how to release and constrain entropy.
- Seven self-governance traits: experimentation, awareness, development, reliance, education, sufficiency, mastery.
- At the start, selfishness is selfless: free yourself, then free others.
