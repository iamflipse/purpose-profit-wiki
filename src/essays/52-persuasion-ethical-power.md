# 52. Persuasion as Ethical Power

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Value Creation  
**Book pages:** p. 93  

## Summary
Koe gives a formula: value is how much people care multiplied by the size of the problems you solve. Power is the degree to which one can change behavior. Force and deception were the old routes, and deception gets caught by reviews and reputation. Persuasion, meaning inspiring people to see how your work improves their lives, is the most ethical form of power, and it aims at positive-sum outcomes.

## Key ideas
- Money is a unit of value.
- Value = how much people care × magnitude of problems solved.
- Power is the degree to which one can change behavior.
- Force and deception are unwise; reputation catches up.
- Persuasion inspires people to see the importance of what you do.
- Persuasion is the most ethical form of power.
- Understanding the other mind enables mutual benefit, a positive-sum game.
