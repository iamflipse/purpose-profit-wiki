# 29. The Four Levels and 1. Survival

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Levels of Purpose  
**Book pages:** pp. 51 to 53  

## Summary
Koe introduces four macro levels of purpose that apply to every domain of life. None is bad; selfishness comes first. In survival you live on assigned goals, think money is luck or evil, and repeat the same day. To advance, become aware of the beliefs holding you back, shift focus from problem to solution, start where you are, and expose yourself to massive experience.

## Key ideas
- Four levels of purpose apply to each domain (work, relationships, finances, health, energy).
- No level is bad; they're developmental stages.
- You solve superficial problems (health, money, confidence) before deeper ones.
- Survival: assigned goals, bills, arguments, repetition.
- Luck describes a lack of understanding of a system.
- Step one out: awareness of limiting beliefs. Step two: focus from problem to solution.
- Start where you are, learn complementary skills, run side projects, get massive experience.
