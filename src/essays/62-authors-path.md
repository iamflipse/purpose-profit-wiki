# 62. The Author's Path

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Self-Monetization  
**Book pages:** pp. 119 to 122  

## Summary
Koe tells his own story. At sixteen he rejected the default path. He spent nearly five years getting good at failure across photography, web design and wallets, took a web design job, and built freelance work until he could leave. He realized he had built himself a new job, so he started writing in public, made digital books and a paid community, and discovered writing was the missing piece. That led to The Art of Focus and later a writing software company.

## Key ideas
- At sixteen he saw the conventional path wasn't for him.
- Nearly five years of becoming good at one thing: failure.
- Persistence turned failures into clarity.
- Freelance web work let him leave his job, but he'd built a new job.
- Writing in public led to digital books, replies and a paid community.
- Writing itself was the missing piece.
- Problems are infinite and soluble; next came a software company.
