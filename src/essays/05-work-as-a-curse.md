# 5. Work as a Curse

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Truth About Jobs  
**Book pages:** p. 10  

## Summary
Koe describes the cycle of craving rest at work and work at rest, and the delusion of retirement. Survival mode makes you skip the habits you promised yourself until your mind, body and relationships unravel.

## Key ideas
- Work now feels like a curse full of stress, overwhelm and anxiety.
- Too much work makes you crave rest and too much rest makes you crave work.
- Retirement is a perpetual vacation that normalizes within weeks.
- We work to rest and end up unsatisfied with how little we are.
- Survival mode means skipping good habits until life unravels into chaos.

## Quotes

> "When you are at work for too long, you crave to be at rest. When you are at rest for too long, you crave to be at work."
