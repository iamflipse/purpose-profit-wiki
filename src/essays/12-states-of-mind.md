# 12. Employee and Entrepreneur Are States of Mind

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Employment vs Entrepreneurship  
**Book pages:** pp. 20 to 21  

## Summary
Being an eternal employee is harder than being an entrepreneur, because of the mind rather than the work. Self-development leads into entrepreneurship because improving others is the next level of improving yourself. Employee and entrepreneur are states of mind, so you can hold a job and still build agency. Your work has to change as you outgrow each level of purpose.

## Key ideas
- Being an eternal employee is harder because of the mind, not the work.
- Self-development is a gateway drug into entrepreneurship.
- Employee and entrepreneur are states of mind, not titles.
- Entrepreneurs set or adopt a vision, learn by curiosity and create solutions.
- As an entrepreneur, problems aren't defined; your path reveals them as you move.
- You can have a job and still cultivate agency, as long as it fuels your vision.
