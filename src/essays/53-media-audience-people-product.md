# 53. Media, Audience, People and Product

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Value Creation  
**Book pages:** pp. 94 to 97  

## Summary
Entrepreneurs persuade growing numbers of people through media, and the internet is the highest-leverage, permissionless place to create. Building your own audience frees you from publishers, labels and employers. You need a product and people who care, and both have to meet. Being outside is less risky than it looks, because staying put is uncertain too. We're wired for tribal approval, which pushes us to contribute value.

## Key ideas
- You persuade growing numbers of people through media.
- The internet is the highest-leverage, permissionless place to create, for now.
- Share value where the right people can find it, whatever the medium.
- Building an audience preserves access to people who trust you.
- Specialists' craft is worthless if nobody knows or cares.
- You need a product and people who care; be in front of the right people.
- Publishing with intention, persistence and iteration increases surface area.
- We're wired to seek tribal approval and contribute value.
