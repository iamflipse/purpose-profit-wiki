# 49. Projects, Constraints and Levers

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Your Life’s Work  
**Book pages:** pp. 87 to 88  

## Summary
Projects turn problems into solutions and become magnets for ideas, serendipity and pattern recognition. Learning comes from struggle, so build first and learn from the gaps that exposes. Constraints, meaning what you refuse to sacrifice, force creativity. Levers are daily priority tasks. If you aren't making progress, you aren't moving levers.

## Key ideas
- Projects turn problems into solutions and give your mind a frame.
- Projects become magnets for ideas; serendipity and pattern recognition increase.
- Learning comes from struggle, not memorization.
- Build, then learn: let the project expose your gaps.
- Constraints force creativity: what won't you sacrifice?
- Levers are daily priority tasks that move projects, goals and vision.
- No progress means you aren't moving levers.
