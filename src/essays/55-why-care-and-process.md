# 55. Where, When, Why, and the Process

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Value Creation  
**Book pages:** pp. 100 to 103  

## Summary
The last three questions. Where do they want to be? Be specific about the transformation. When will they get results? Clarity and honesty make the best marketing, and a timeframe adds structure. Why should they care? Stack pains and benefits until you reach the core. You solve the problem with a process built through experimentation, and the process is the product. Answer all five and the offer becomes irresistible.

## Key ideas
- Where: the desired outcome and specific transformation.
- Specificity generates desire: 'ten less negative thoughts by tomorrow'.
- When: clarity paired with honesty is the greatest marketing strategy.
- Behavior change is the only metric that matters.
- Why: a stack of whys built from pains and benefits.
- Your process is the product; build it through experimentation.
- All five questions together make an offer irresistible.
