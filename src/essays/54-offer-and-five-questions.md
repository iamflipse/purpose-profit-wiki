# 54. Offers, Perception and the First Two Questions

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Value Creation  
**Book pages:** pp. 97 to 99  

## Summary
Media and product have to be packaged as an offer: value exchanged for money or for attention. Value is perception, as the t-shirt and the Rolex show. Koe poses five questions for shaping perception. First, who can you help most? Personality drives perception, and the best person to help is yourself. Second, what problem are you solving? The problem always comes first, and you meet people at their level of awareness.

## Key ideas
- You need people and a product, attracted with media and packaged as an offer.
- You offer value in exchange for value: money for products, attention for media.
- Value is perception; different people value different things.
- Five questions: who, what problem, where, when, why.
- Personality is the largest influence on perception; help your past self.
- The problem always comes first; it hooks the reader.
- Levels of awareness: unaware, problem aware, solution aware, product aware, most aware.
