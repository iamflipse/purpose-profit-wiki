# 46. The Mind Is a Story Engine

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Your Life’s Work  
**Book pages:** pp. 83 to 84  

## Summary
Your psyche wants actualization, but your ego is distracted and you lack a plan with more gravity than your distractions. Koe sets out to show how to become valuable and how to create a plan, and suggests reading twice, once for yourself and once for your customers. The mind is a story engine: create a story worth telling and attracting others becomes trivial.

## Key ideas
- The psyche craves actualization; the ego is distracted.
- You need a plan with more gravity than your distractions.
- Short-term stress tasks crowd out a long time horizon.
- Read the book twice: for yourself and for your customers.
- The mind is a story engine; humans make sense of the world in stories.
- Leaders attract followers, and leaders still follow leaders.
