# 44. A Plan Is Your Surface Area for Luck

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Your Life’s Work  
**Book pages:** pp. 81 to 82  

## Summary
You need a way to course-correct, not wander the desert. A plan is your surface area for luck, and not having one is still a plan. It isn't a list of steps but an evolving blueprint shaped by trial and error, with vision, mission, projects and levers. Nobody can tell you how to achieve your plan, only how they achieved theirs.

## Key ideas
- You need a smart way to course-correct.
- A plan is your surface area for luck.
- Not having a plan is still a plan.
- A plan is an evolving blueprint, not a list of steps.
- A plan contains the rules of the game for how you live.
- A plan prevents overwhelm and governs how you release and constrain entropy.
- Others can only tell you how they achieved their plan.
