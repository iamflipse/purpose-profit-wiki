# 33. The Meta Path: Your Life's Work

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Levels of Purpose  
**Book pages:** pp. 61 to 62  

## Summary
No one can give you the path, but there is a meta path: commit to discovering and pursuing your life's work across mind, body, relationships and work. Work determines much of your development. When work demands your best self, health and relationships become non-negotiable. Entrepreneurship spans all levels, demands generalism, and requires you to escape starving artistry.

## Key ideas
- The best path is the one you create; there's a meta path to create it.
- Main domains: mind, body, relationships and work.
- Work determines much of your time and development.
- A job can be a stepping-stone but chains you to responsibilities.
- When work demands your best self, you must uphold mind, body and relationships.
- Entrepreneurship solves survival, aids status, allows creativity, results in contribution.
- It requires generalism and escaping starving artistry.
