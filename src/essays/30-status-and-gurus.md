# 30. 2. Status, Power and the Gurus

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Levels of Purpose  
**Book pages:** pp. 53 to 56  

## Summary
Status is the second level. Koe says power and influence have always mattered, and even the great teachers had status through a message rather than a marketing budget. It is the duty of the strong to bear responsibility. Many gurus developed contemplative depth but neglected body, money and ethics. Status is reached when you feel secure about money, and the trap is getting stuck in materialism or trying to skip the stage.

## Key ideas
- Power and influence have always separated who did well.
- Wealth is the ability to create.
- Great teachers had a message, not a marketing budget.
- It's the duty of the strong to bear the load of responsibility.
- Many gurus failed to integrate the descending: body, money, ethics.
- Status means security around money; the trap is getting stuck in the material.
- Skipping status to jump to spirituality turns spirituality into a status symbol.
- Koe gives permission to pursue acceptance through power, consciously.
