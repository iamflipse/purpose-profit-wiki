# 35. Wiener's Warning and Universal Explainers

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Progress and Knowledge  
**Book pages:** pp. 65 to 67  

## Summary
Wiener warned that the danger is humans being treated like machines, not machines becoming human. Commercial incentives and John McCarthy buried the word cybernetics under 'artificial intelligence'. Koe answers 'what makes humans special' with David Deutsch: we create infinite knowledge through conjecture and criticism, and as universal explainers we can understand anything understandable.

## Key ideas
- Wiener: 'We must cease to kiss the whip that lashes us.'
- The danger is humans treated like machines.
- Commercial incentives and McCarthy replaced cybernetics with 'artificial intelligence'.
- All knowledge is created through conjecture and criticism: guessing and correcting.
- Humans are universal explainers who can understand anything understandable.
- Explanatory theories let us understand what we never directly experience.
