# 16. Money Haters and Free Things

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Unignorability of Money  
**Book pages:** pp. 27 to 28  

## Summary
People who demonize money often aren't in control of their own minds. They resent corporations and turn on creators who try to earn. Free things aren't taken seriously: when people pay, they pay attention. If you don't create a product, you sell someone else's or become the product. Sales and persuasion are not manipulation when developed people use them to educate and transform.

## Key ideas
- Money beliefs are passed down from parents, teachers and society.
- Money-haters despise corporations while ignoring that they provide some value.
- Supporters often turn on creators the minute they try to earn.
- Free things aren't taken seriously; when people pay, they pay attention.
- Value lies in transformation, not commodity.
- If you don't create a product to sell, you sell one for someone else or become the product.
- Sales and persuasion educate and transform when used by developed individuals.
