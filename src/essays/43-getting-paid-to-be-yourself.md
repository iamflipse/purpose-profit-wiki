# 43. Getting Paid to Be Yourself

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Your Life’s Work  
**Book pages:** pp. 79 to 81  

## Summary
Your life's work is reaching your potential and getting paid to be yourself, which is profiting from purpose. Clarity doesn't arrive in an instant. You go from lost to curious to intense to consistent, spiralling up. Instead of obsessing over finding your life's work, look at where your current actions lead, because entropy means doing nothing is a slow drowning.

## Key ideas
- Your life's work is to reach your potential and see what you're capable of.
- It means getting paid to be yourself: profiting from purpose.
- The alternative is working only for survival and status.
- The question isn't 'what do I do?' but 'which way do I go?'
- The path: lost, faith, curiosity, intensity, consistency, a new level.
- Look at where your life ends up if you keep the same actions.
- Learn how to think, learn and earn: make a goal conscious, plan, focus on lever-moving actions.
