# 15. What Money Is

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Unignorability of Money  
**Book pages:** pp. 25 to 26  

## Summary
Demonizing money stunts your growth. Money breaks down into currency (a medium of exchange) and capital (a store of value). Here it is a neutral measure of value whose perceived worth depends on the problems and goals framing your mind. People project insecurities onto money that were passed down to them and that they can fix.

## Key ideas
- Demonizing money is the fastest way to stunt growth.
- Your view of money is usually social conditioning.
- Money is currency (exchange) and capital (store of value); the book focuses on currency.
- Money is a neutral measure of value between a person and a good or service.
- What you value depends on the problems and goals framing your mind.
- Beliefs and insecurities about money are passed down and are not permanent; problems are soluble.
