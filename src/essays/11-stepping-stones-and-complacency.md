# 11. Stepping-Stones and Complacency

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Employment vs Entrepreneurship  
**Book pages:** pp. 18 to 20  

## Summary
Schools and jobs are useful stepping-stones for skills, status and exposure, but they breed complacency and go against our need for uncertainty and challenge. Once people hit the ceiling they justify comfort, and the calling disappears. Since work fills a third of life, stagnation there spills into everything. Entrepreneurship means loving the endless string of problems that widen your circle of concern.

## Key ideas
- Schools and jobs are stepping-stones for skills, status and exposure.
- They breed complacency and go against our need for uncertainty, challenge and improvement.
- When the ladder runs out, people justify comfort with the 'stability' line, and the calling disappears.
- Your other domains can only rise as high as your ability to contribute through work.
- Stagnation in work makes a decline into chaos inevitable.
- There is no final goal that lets you opt out of work; fall in love with the endless problems instead.
- Entrepreneurship is the path of uncertainty, failure, rejection and slow progress.
