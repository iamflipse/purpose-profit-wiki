# 42. Experiment Inward, Outward, Downward, Upward

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Progress and Knowledge  
**Book pages:** pp. 75 to 78  

## Summary
The cycle repeats at a higher level. When lost, experiment inward (rest, journal, walk, silence) and outward (learn, read, build, try things). Once interested, experiment downward: go deep, gather every perspective and avoid dogma. Then experiment upward: build a project, write in public, sell it, improve it and evolve. Keep one foot in the unknown with a vision and an anti-vision.

## Key ideas
- Each cycle ends and a new one begins at a deeper level.
- Experiment inward: rest, nap, journal, walk, silence, contemplation.
- Experiment outward: learn, read, build, try new things.
- No time limit on being lost; stick it out until enough vision accumulates.
- Experiment downward: go deep, collect all perspectives, avoid dogma.
- Obsessive self-experimentation is the only way to solve problems for good.
- Experiment upward: build, write in public, sell, improve, evolve.
- Keep one foot in the unknown, framed by vision and anti-vision.
