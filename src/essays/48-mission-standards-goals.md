# 48. Mission, Standards and Goals

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Your Life’s Work  
**Book pages:** pp. 85 to 86  

## Summary
Mission bridges anti-vision and vision and requires faith, because you can't see the next step until you take one. Standards decide what registers as a problem, and they are absorbed from your environment, so change the environment. Big goals give direction and small goals give clarity. Break the vision into goals by decade down to day, and stay stubborn on vision but loose on details.

## Key ideas
- Mission is the bridge between what you do and don't want.
- Your mission requires faith; the next step appears after the first.
- You aren't where you want to be because you're okay with where you are.
- Standards are mostly unconscious and absorbed from environment.
- Your old identity won't go down without a fight.
- Change your physical, mental and spiritual environments.
- Big goals for direction, small goals for clarity; goals come after vision.
