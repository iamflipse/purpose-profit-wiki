# 18. Money as a Skill Tree

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Unignorability of Money  
**Book pages:** pp. 30 to 31  

## Summary
Almost every action has money attached, and pretending otherwise is self-deception. Like a skill tree in a game, money unlocks new paths: health, relationships, building. Koe doubts the fortune-cookie idea that money stops helping after a few million, because money buys the resources to keep solving harder problems and to pass down solutions.

## Key ideas
- Almost every action has money attached as a reason; denying it is self-deception.
- Money works like a skill tree that unlocks new paths.
- Building requires resources and resources require money.
- Enough is not much for most people, but how money is made matters.
- The 'more money won't help' idea is a shallow half-truth.
- Money's best use is a life of novelty and challenge, buying resources for harder problems.
