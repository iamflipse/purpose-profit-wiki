# 22. Humans Were Never Meant to Be Tools

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Deep Generalism  
**Book pages:** p. 37  

## Summary
Humans are natural-born generalists who build tools to survive any environment. Koe argues we were never supposed to become the tools, which is why people fear replacement. A tool that costs less and never sleeps becomes a profit machine only for people with the vision, agency and knowledge to apply it.

## Key ideas
- Humans are creators and natural-born generalists meant to master many domains.
- We built tools to escape the niches that keep animals in place.
- We were never supposed to become the tools.
- People fear replacement because they aren't the ones doing the replacing.
- Cheap, tireless tools are profit machines only for those with vision, agency and knowledge.
