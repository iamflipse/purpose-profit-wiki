# 57. Start Writing: The Meta-Skill

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Meta Skill  
**Book pages:** pp. 108 to 110  

## Summary
The world needs more creators who pass down knowledge. Koe's advice: if you're unsure what skill to learn, start writing. Writing teaches you how to learn, think and earn. It is a meta-skill, and writing in public brings feedback that improves thinking and teaches psychology, marketing and persuasion along the way. With AI handling technical work, taste, agency and coherence remain, and writing is the permissionless leverage behind media and, increasingly, code.

## Key ideas
- People need powerful ideas more than assignments.
- To create is to pass down knowledge and contribute to cumulative progress.
- The mark of a free individual is doing many things.
- Writing checks three boxes: learning, thinking, earning.
- Writing in public exposes you to feedback and error correction.
- With AI, what's left is taste, agency, coherence.
- Writing is permissionless leverage; it underlies media and now code.
- Writing will be the cornerstone habit of successful work.
