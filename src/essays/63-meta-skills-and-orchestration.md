# 63. Your Story, Meta Skills and Orchestration

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** Self-Monetization  
**Book pages:** pp. 122 to 125  

## Summary
The path isn't limited to information products. People solve their own problems with glasses, clothing or soap, and an old product plus your story plus the internet beats competition. Jordan Peterson shows how a mind, unlike time or labor, can be distributed. Single skills keep getting automated, so humans abstract up to meta skills, wearing several hats tied together with taste and coherence and orchestrating tools toward a vision.

## Key ideas
- Not limited to information: physical products solving your own problems work too.
- Old product + your story + internet overcomes competition.
- Peterson uses whatever tools let him pursue his life's work.
- A mind, unlike time or labor, can be distributed globally.
- Single skills have always been replaced by machines.
- Each round of evolution abstracts humans up to meta skills.
- Humans must orchestrate tools toward an evolving vision.
