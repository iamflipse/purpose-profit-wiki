# 6. Problems: Chosen or Assigned

[The big picture](../index.md) | [Cheat sheet](../cheatsheet.md) | [Concepts](../concepts.md) | [Themes](../themes.md)

**Essay:** The Truth About Jobs  
**Book pages:** pp. 10 to 11  

## Summary
Work is energy invested in solving a problem. Chosen problems focus attention and dissolve worry, while assigned problems enslave attention. Purpose can't exist without problems: your purpose is the beginning of your suffering, and you can choose what you suffer for.

## Key ideas
- Work is energy invested in solving a problem.
- The right problems narrow attention and make you forget your worries.
- The difference between good and bad problems is whether they are chosen or assigned.
- Problems are the limits on your mind; solving them allows growth.
- Purpose and problems are bound together; you choose what you suffer for.
- Create work that doesn't feel like work, however hard.
